Preferred Product

We feature CARPRO Nano Diamond Quartz as our preferred tropical‑environment option for hardness, gloss, slickness, thermal stability, and salt/fallout resistance.

Other options may include CARPRO CQUARTZ DQUARTZ, SiC, UK 3.0, and Lite 2.0; Gyeon Q2 Mohs EVO, Pure EVO, and One EVO; IGL Ecocoat Quartz+; and Kamikaze ISM 2.0.

Durability depends on product grade, preparation, UV, heat, salt, outdoor parking, maintenance, and tropical exposure — we do not promise a universal lifespan.

Conditions to Know

  • Repainted panels generally require 30–90 days to cure before coating.
  • Deep scratches and etched water spots may remain unless corrected.
  • Failing, peeling, or thin clear coat may be declined or restricted to non‑abrasive work.
  • Racks, running boards, pickup beds, textured trim, lifted suspensions, and accessories can increase cost.
  • Final pricing is confirmed after inspection under detailing lights.
