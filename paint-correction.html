Paint Enhancement & Correction

Restore Clarity. Revive Gloss. Correct Imperfections.

Paint correction is the controlled process of refining the upper paint surface to reduce visible defects and restore clarity, gloss, depth, and reflectivity. The goal is the best visual improvement while preserving healthy clear coat.

Inspection Should Assess

  • Clear‑coat health and thickness
  • Scratch depth and defect severity
  • Oxidation, peeling, cracking, or delamination
  • Previous paintwork and body repairs
  • Paint hardness, contamination, thin edges, and body lines
  • Existing ceramic coatings, sealants, PPF, or vinyl

Professional Process

Inspection, thorough rinse, safe hand wash, wheel and tire cleaning.

Chemical, iron, fallout, tar, sap, adhesive, and clay decontamination as needed.

Final rinse, drying, crevice blowout, and inspection under detailing lights.

Test spot, machine compounding when appropriate, finishing polish and refinement.

Panel‑by‑panel review, final residue removal, and selected protection application.

Honest Results, Safe Correction

  • Deep scratches through clear coat cannot safely be polished away.
  • Rock chips, dents, rust, missing paint, deep gouges, and failing clear coat may require repair.
  • Thin, peeling, cracking, or delaminating clear coat may be unsuitable for aggressive correction.
  • Some deeper defects may remain to preserve clear‑coat integrity.
  • Safe professional improvement takes priority over 100% defect removal.
