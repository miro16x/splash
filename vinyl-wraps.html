Before You Wrap

  • Weak, repaired, or failing paint may lift during removal; paint‑safe removal cannot be guaranteed.
  • Tropical UV and heat can make old vinyl and adhesive harder to remove.
  • Vehicles with severe oxidation, rust, peeling clear coat, loose trim, or major damage may be declined.
  • Please disclose repainting, bodywork, prior wraps, coatings, PPF, and known damage.
