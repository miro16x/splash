Paint Enhancement & Correction

Restore Clarity. Revive Gloss. Correct Imperfections.

Paint correction is the controlled process of refining the upper paint surface to reduce visible defects and restore clarity, gloss, depth, and reflectivity. The goal is the best visual improvement while preserving healthy clear coat.

Enhancement / One‑Step Polish

Improves gloss while reducing light swirls and haze.

One‑Step Compound + Polish

Combines cutting and refinement when appropriate.

Stage 1 Correction

Targets wash marks, oxidation, swirls, and moderate defects — approximately 50–70% visible defect reduction when condition allows.

Stage 2 Correction

Intensive multi‑step correction and refinement — approximately 80–90% visible defect reduction when safely possible.

Heavy / Custom Correction

Multiple cutting and finishing stages, limited by safe clear‑coat thickness.

Protect the Result

Correction restores the finish; protection preserves it. We recommend ceramic coating, paint sealant, or wax after polishing.

Inspection Should Assess

  • Clear‑coat health and thickness
  • Scratch depth and defect severity
  • Oxidation, peeling, cracking, or delamination
  • Previous paintwork and body repairs
  • Paint hardness, contamination, thin edges, and body lines
  • Existing ceramic coatings, sealants, PPF, or vinyl

Professional Process

Inspection, thorough rinse, safe hand wash, wheel and tire cleaning.

Chemical, iron, fallout, tar, sap, adhesive, and clay decontamination as needed.

Final rinse, drying, crevice blowout, and inspection under detailing lights.

Test spot, machine compounding when appropriate, finishing polish and refinement.

Panel‑by‑panel review, final residue removal, and selected protection application.

Honest Results, Safe Correction

  • Deep scratches through clear coat cannot safely be polished away.
  • Rock chips, dents, rust, missing paint, deep gouges, and failing clear coat may require repair.
  • Thin, peeling, cracking, or delaminating clear coat may be unsuitable for aggressive correction.
  • Some deeper defects may remain to preserve clear‑coat integrity.
  • Safe professional improvement takes priority over 100% defect removal.

Aftercare

Keep your results looking sharp in island conditions

Sun, salt air, and sand are hard on a fresh finish — here's how to protect your investment by service.

  • Remove bird droppings, sap, bugs, and contamination promptly in tropical heat.
  • Rinse salt and sand off regularly if parked near the water.
  • Dry with clean microfiber or forced air to avoid water‑spotting.
  • Ask about an annual coating inspection and a printable aftercare guide at your next visit.
  • Avoid detergent washing and harsh chemicals for the first 7–14 days.
  • Hand‑wash with pH‑neutral shampoo without added wax; avoid automatic brushes.
  • Dry with clean microfiber or forced air.
  • Remove bird droppings, sap, bugs, and contamination promptly in tropical heat.
  • Follow the film manufacturer's instructions and avoid aggressive washing while adhesive stabilizes.
  • Hand‑wash gently; avoid brushes and direct high pressure at edges, seams, corners, or lifted areas.
  • Avoid abrasive compounds, harsh solvents, and aggressive degreasers.
  • Use shade or covered storage when possible, and contact us if an edge begins lifting.
