Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Splash & Dash Car Wash & Auto Detail Center (“Splash & Dash,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use our website, request a quote, or contact us.

Information we collect

When you submit a quote request, you may provide your name, email address, phone number, vehicle details, requested service, preferred date, notes, selected options, and photographs. If you contact us directly, we receive the information included in your message.

Our site may also receive basic technical information automatically, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, referring page, and access time, through our hosting and third-party service providers. We store your light or dark theme preference in your browser’s local storage. It is not sent to us by that feature.

How we use information

  • To respond to inquiries and prepare, schedule, and manage service estimates or appointments.
  • To inspect a vehicle’s condition and recommend suitable services.
  • To provide customer service and communicate about requested work.
  • To operate, protect, troubleshoot, and improve our website and business.
  • To comply with law, prevent fraud, and protect our rights and safety.

The maintenance-reminder sign-up displayed on the current site does not transmit or retain the entered email address. If we activate that feature later, we will update this policy and provide any required consent or unsubscribe controls.

How information is shared

Quote forms are processed by FormSubmit, which delivers the submitted information to us. Our site also uses Google Fonts and an embedded Google Map; when those resources load, Google may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser details. Those providers process information under their own privacy terms.

We may also share information with service providers that help us host or operate the site, when required by law or a valid legal process, or when reasonably necessary to protect our customers, business, or others. We do not sell personal information.

Retention and security

We retain personal information only as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described above, including customer service, business records, dispute resolution, and legal obligations. We use reasonable safeguards, but no website, email, or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices

You may ask to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you, subject to applicable law and legitimate recordkeeping needs. You can block local storage or third-party resources in your browser, although parts of the site may not work as intended.

Children’s privacy

Our website and services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our practices or legal obligations change. The effective date above shows when the latest version took effect.
