FAQ

Common questions, answered plainly

Learn what to expect when booking car care with Splash & Dash in St. Thomas.

How do I book an appointment or request a quote?

Use the Request a Quote form. The fields adjust based on the service you select. Most services need a quick in-person inspection or clear photos before we can confirm final pricing. A submitted request is not an appointment until our team confirms it.

Why can’t I get an instant price online?

Paint correction, ceramic coating, and vinyl wrap pricing depend on your vehicle’s size, condition, and existing surface state. We publish honest starting prices, then confirm the final number after inspection so there are no surprises.

Can paint correction remove every scratch?

No. Correction safely reduces swirls, oxidation, and light-to-moderate defects, but deep scratches through the clear coat cannot be polished away without risking the clear coat’s integrity. We prioritize safe, honest improvement over removing every defect.

How long does a ceramic coating last?

Durability depends on the product grade, preparation, UV exposure, heat, salt, outdoor parking, and how the vehicle is maintained afterward—especially in a tropical climate. Ask us during your consultation for guidance specific to your vehicle.

Is vinyl wrap removal guaranteed to be paint-safe?

Not always. Weak, repaired, or failing paint can lift during removal, and tropical UV and heat can make older vinyl and adhesive harder to remove cleanly. We inspect before quoting removal and flag any risk we find.

What should I remove before my appointment?

Please remove valuables and personal belongings, especially from areas we will clean. Tell us about loose trim, leaks, sensitive electronics, aftermarket parts, previous paint repairs, or other known issues before work begins.

What is your cancellation and weather policy?

Please contact us promptly if you need to reschedule or cancel. We may reschedule because of severe weather, unsafe conditions, equipment issues, or circumstances outside our control. Any cancellation fee or deposit terms will be disclosed before you confirm a paid booking.

What if I have a concern after service?

Please inspect your vehicle at pickup and tell us promptly about any concern. We will evaluate it in good faith and, when appropriate, offer a reasonable correction of the affected work. Specific warranties must be provided in writing.
