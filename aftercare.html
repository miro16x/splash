Aftercare

Keep your results looking sharp in island conditions

Sun, salt air, and sand are hard on a fresh finish — here's how to protect your investment by service.

  • Remove bird droppings, sap, bugs, and contamination promptly in tropical heat.
  • Rinse salt and sand off regularly if parked near the water.
  • Dry with clean microfiber or forced air to avoid water‑spotting.
  • Ask about an annual coating inspection and a printable aftercare guide at your next visit.
  • Avoid detergent washing and harsh chemicals for the first 7–14 days.
  • Hand‑wash with pH‑neutral shampoo without added wax; avoid automatic brushes.
  • Dry with clean microfiber or forced air.
  • Remove bird droppings, sap, bugs, and contamination promptly in tropical heat.
  • Follow the film manufacturer's instructions and avoid aggressive washing while adhesive stabilizes.
  • Hand‑wash gently; avoid brushes and direct high pressure at edges, seams, corners, or lifted areas.
  • Avoid abrasive compounds, harsh solvents, and aggressive degreasers.
  • Use shade or covered storage when possible, and contact us if an edge begins lifting.
